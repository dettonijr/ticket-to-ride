# Ticket to Ride

The vocabulary of the game as played on the classic USA map. Terms follow the 2015 Days of Wonder rulebook where it names something, and pick one word where it does not.

## Board

**City**:
A named point on the board that routes connect. The USA map has 36.

**Route**:
A claimable segment of consecutive spaces joining two adjacent cities, with a length and a colour.
_Avoid_: Track, path, connection, link, edge

**Double Route**:
Two parallel routes joining the same two cities. One player may never claim both.

**Grey Route**:
A route with no colour, claimable with a set of train cards of any one colour.

**Space**:
One slot on a route that holds one train. A route's length is its number of spaces.

## Cards

**Train Card**:
A hand card in one of the eight colours (purple, blue, orange, white, green, yellow, black, red) used to claim routes.
_Avoid_: Train car card, wagon, colour card

**Locomotive**:
The wild train card that may stand in for any colour.
_Avoid_: Wild, joker, rainbow

**Face-up Cards**:
The five train cards lying visible beside the deck that any player may draw from.
_Avoid_: Market, display, river

**Ticket**:
A card naming two cities and a point value, scored at the end of the game as a bonus if the cities are connected by the holder's routes and as a penalty if not.
_Avoid_: Destination, destination card, mission, objective, route

## Play

**Train**:
One of a player's 45 coloured pieces, placed one per space when claiming a route.
_Avoid_: Car, wagon, piece, token

**Claim**:
The act of playing a matching set of train cards to take a route and place trains on it.
_Avoid_: Build, buy, take, occupy

**Turn**:
One player's single action: draw train cards, claim a route, or draw tickets.

**Final Round**:
The last turn for every player, triggered when any player ends a turn with two or fewer trains.

**Longest Path**:
The longest continuous chain of one player's trains, counted by spaces, where no train is used twice but a city may be passed more than once. Worth a ten point bonus shared by all players tied for it.
_Avoid_: Longest route, longest train
